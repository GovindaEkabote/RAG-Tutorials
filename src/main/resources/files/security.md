# Information Security Policy

## 1. Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems or data.

## 2. Data Classification
| Level      | Description                          | Examples                      |
|------------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Public     | Safe for public release              | Marketing materials           |
| Internal   | Internal use only                    | Org charts, policies          |
| Confidential | Business-sensitive                 | Financials, contracts         |
| Restricted | Regulated / highly sensitive         | PII, credentials, health data |

- **Restricted** data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## 3. Acceptable Use
- Company devices are for business use; limited personal use is permitted.
- Do not install unauthorized software or bypass security controls.
- Do not use company email for illegal, harassing, or discriminatory content.

## 4. Incident Response
- Report incidents (phishing, lost device, breach) to **security@company.com** or ext. 5911 **immediately**.
- Do not attempt to investigate or remediate alone.
- Preserve evidence; do not delete logs or files.

## 5. Phishing & Social Engineering
- Verify sender addresses and links before clicking.
- Report suspicious emails using the **Report Phishing** button.
- IT will never ask for your password via email or phone.

## 6. Access Control
- Follow **least privilege** and **need-to-know** principles.
- Access reviews are conducted quarterly.
- Access must be revoked within 24 hours of termination.

## 7. Encryption & Devices
- Full-disk encryption is mandatory on all laptops.
- Removable media must be company-approved and encrypted.
- Lock your screen when leaving your desk (Win+L / Ctrl+Cmd+Q).

## 8. Compliance
- Comply with GDPR, HIPAA (where applicable), and local regulations.
- Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination.
