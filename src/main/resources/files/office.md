# Office Facilities & Workplace

## 1. Locations
- **HQ:** 100 Main Street, City Center
- **Branch:** 45 Tech Park, Innovation District

## 2. Access & Badges
- Badges are issued by Facilities on day one.
- Lost badges must be reported immediately; replacement fee: $25.
- Do not tailgate or lend badges to visitors.

## 3. Desks & Meeting Rooms
- Hot-desking applies on floors 3–5; reserve via the **Room Booking System**.
- Meeting rooms must be released if unused after **15 minutes**.
- Keep calls in phone booths or designated zones.

## 4. Parking
- Available on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Reserved spots require a permit from Facilities.
- EV charging stations: 4 spots on Level B1.

## 5. Kitchen & Supplies
- Coffee, tea, and snacks are provided on each floor.
- Label personal food; unlabeled items are discarded on Fridays.
- Clean up after yourself; report spills to Facilities ext. 5600.

## 6. Mail & Packages
- Deliveries are received at the mailroom (Ground Floor).
- Notifications are sent when packages arrive.
- Personal deliveries should be limited.

## 7. Maintenance
- Report facility issues via the Facilities Portal or ext. 5600.
- Emergency (water leak, power outage): ext. 5911.

## 8. Dress Code
- Business casual Monday–Thursday.
- Casual Friday is permitted (no beachwear).
- Client-facing days: business formal.
