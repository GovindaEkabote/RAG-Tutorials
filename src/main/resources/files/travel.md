# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Approval
- All business travel requires **manager approval** before booking.
- International travel also requires **VP approval**.
- Submit requests at least **14 days** in advance (domestic) and **30 days** (international).

## 2. Booking
- Book through the **Corporate Travel Portal** only.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for 6+ hours.
- Business class requires Director approval.
- Use preferred hotel and car rental vendors for negotiated rates.

## 3. Expenses
| Category        | Limit                         |
|-----------------|-------------------------------|
| Hotel           | $250/night (domestic)         |
| Meals           | $60/day (domestic)            |
| Meals           | $90/day (international)       |
| Ground transport| Reasonable / actual cost      |

- Itemized receipts are required for all expenses over $25.
- Submit expense reports within **30 days** of trip completion.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment with prior approval.

## 4. Air Travel
- Book **21+ days** in advance when possible.
- Frequent flyer miles may be accrued personally; upgrades at personal cost.
- Cancel unused flights promptly to avoid fees.

## 5. Ground Transportation
- Prefer airport shuttles or public transport where practical.
- Ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft) is permitted; choose standard tiers.
- Rental cars require prior approval and must be compact/mid-size unless justified.

## 6. International Travel
- Check visa and passport validity (6+ months remaining).
- Register your trip in the **Travel Safety Portal**.
- Ensure travel insurance is active; policy details in the portal.
- Follow local laws and customs; report safety concerns to security@company.com.

## 7. Personal Travel Combined with Business
- Personal days may be added at your own expense.
- Any cost difference must be clearly documented and approved.

## 8. Emergencies
- Contact the **24/7 Travel Assistance Line:** +1-800-555-0199.
- Notify your manager and HR of any medical or security incidents.
