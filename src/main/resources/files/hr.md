# Human Resources (HR) Policy

## 1. Working Hours
- Standard working hours are **9:00 AM – 6:00 PM**, Monday to Friday.
- A one-hour lunch break is included.
- Flexible start times (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM) are permitted with manager approval.

## 2. Leave Policy
| Leave Type       | Days per Year | Notes                          |
|------------------|---------------|--------------------------------|
| Annual Leave     | 20            | Accrued monthly                |
| Sick Leave       | 10            | Medical certificate if > 2 days|
| Casual Leave     | 5             | Max 2 consecutive days         |
| Parental Leave   | 90            | Paid, per child                |
| Bereavement      | 5             | Immediate family               |

- Leave requests must be submitted **at least 3 working days** in advance via the HR portal.
- Unused annual leave can be carried over up to **5 days** into the next year.

## 3. Payroll
- Salaries are paid on the **last working day** of each month.
- Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 1st of the following month.
- Queries about payroll must be raised within **30 days** of the payslip date.

## 4. Performance Reviews
- Conducted **twice a year** (mid-year and annual).
- Ratings range from 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding).
- Promotions are considered during the annual review cycle.

## 5. Onboarding
- New employees receive an onboarding pack on day one.
- A 90-day probation period applies to all new hires.
- Buddy assignment is mandatory for the first 30 days.

## 6. Exit Process
- Submit resignation with **60 days' notice**.
- Exit interviews are mandatory.
- Full and final settlement is processed within **45 days**.

## 7. Contact
- HR Helpdesk: hr@company.com
- HR Portal: https://hr.company.com
